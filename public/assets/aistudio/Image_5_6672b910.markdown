## Brand & Style

The design system projects immediate authority, speed, and modern transparency. It removes the bureaucratic friction of enterprise hiring platforms in favor of direct, peer-to-peer recruiter connection (inspired by direct-chat hiring platforms like Boss Zhipin and Tal Boss). The interface prioritizes high-velocity decision making: salaries are immediately legible, hiring managers have direct identities, and candidate matching is crisp and confident.

The aesthetic fuses **Modern Executive Direct-Hire** with **Tactile Minimalist Utility**:
- **Tone:** Authoritative, energetic, transparent, and unfiltered.
- **Atmosphere:** High-clarity white canvases, structural charcoal line-work, high-signal emerald and electric mint status indicators, and confident typographic rhythm.
- **Emotional Impact:** Eliminates applicant anxiety by visually surfacing instant feedback loops, direct executive presence ("Direct Boss", "Verified Manager"), and salary transparency.

## Colors

The palette leverages extreme value contrast. Neutral blacks and charcoals command the informational hierarchy, while saturated direct-hire emerald and energetic mint provide unequivocal focus on salaries, active hiring statuses, and verified executive credentials.

- **Primary (`#059669` / Emerald Accent):** Drives high-intent interactive states, verified recruiter tags, active response metrics, and primary confirmation flows.
- **Secondary (`#0F172A` / Slate Black):** Grounds all primary structural headers, high-salary metrics, and prominent job titles to ensure unmistakable visual weight.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981` / Electric Mint):** Reserved for real-time status pulses ("Online now", "Replies within 1 hr"), success badges, and high-visibility compensation bands.
- **Neutral (`#6B7280` / Muted Graphite):** Carries secondary metadata, corporate location pins, company scale indicators, and timestamps.
- **Base Surfaces & Strokes:** Primary workspace canvas rests on stark `#FFFFFF` accented with container tiers of `#F9FAFB` and `#F3F4F6`. Structural division relies on calibrated border strokes (`#E5E7EB` to `#E2E8F0`).

## Typography

The type system pairs the sharp geometric balance of **Hanken Grotesk** with technical, high-signal data markers in **JetBrains Mono**.

- **Editorial Stance:** Headings deliver punchy, condensed energy with tight tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to mimic executive briefing boards.
- **Compensation & Figures:** `label-salary` commands instant visual hierarchy on candidate cards, utilizing ultra-heavy weights in contrast to ambient job descriptions.
- **Executive Badges:** Micro-badges ("Direct Boss", "Verified Manager", "No ATS") leverage `JetBrains Mono` in uppercase styling to evoke audit-grade verification, system transparency, and precision.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around dense, high-utility scanability paired with deliberate breathing room for key interactions:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** Employs a 12-column responsive grid with a 1280px max-width container, 32px gutters (`gutter-lg`), and 48px canvas margins (`margin-lg`). Two-to-three column split workspaces accommodate simultaneous candidate feed browsing and active direct chat panes.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** Collapses to an 8-column grid with 24px gutters (`gutter-md`) and 32px canvas margins (`margin-md`). Sidebar navigation collapses to a compact icon strip.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** Uses a single-column fluid layout with 16px lateral canvas margins (`margin`) and 16px element gaps (`gutter`). Feed density is tuned for rapid vertical thumb scanning, placing executive avatars, salary tags, and direct messaging triggers within immediate reach.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects excessive blur gradients, relying instead on clean, layered structural planes and crisp hairline delineation:

- **Base Surfaces:** Ground level canvas rests at `#FFFFFF`. Grouped listing items and background panels sit on tonal `#F9FAFB` surfaces.
- **Borders & Dividers:** Surfaces are separated by 1px solid hairline borders in `#E5E7EB` (unselected) and `#111827` (focused/active).
- **Shadow Profiles:** Shadows remain restrained, ambient, and tinted with deep slate (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` to `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`):
  - *Resting Card:* 1px border `#E5E7EB` with `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
  - *Hover / Card Focus:* 1px border `#D1D5DB` with `0 8px 20px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07)`.
  - *Floating Modals / Direct Message Drawers:* 1px border `#E5E7EB` with `0 20px 30px -10px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The geometric framework balances approachable modern ergonomics with rigorous executive efficiency:

- **Primary Cards & Containers (`rounded-lg` / 16px):** Job listing cards, profile panels, and communication drawers use generous 16px radii to soften dense informational clusters.
- **Pill Badges & Chips (`rounded-full` / 9999px):** All status tags, salary badges, skill pills, and response-time indicators utilize strict pill shapes to create visual separation from rectilinear cards.
- **Inputs & Buttons (`rounded-md` / 8px to 10px):** Actionable triggers maintain slightly firmer rounding for tactical responsiveness and desktop data precision.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("Direct Chat" / "Apply to Boss"):** High-contrast background in `#059669` or solid `#0F172A`, bold white typography (`Hanken Grotesk`, 600), 8px border-radius, zero shadow, slight scale feedback on press (`0.98`).
- **Secondary / Ghost:** `#FFFFFF` background with 1px border `#E5E7EB`, text `#111827`, subtle hover background fill `#F9FAFB`.
- **Icon Action Buttons:** Circular or 8px rounded containers housing instant call/chat triggers with active green status indicators.

### Cards (Job Listing & Executive Profile)
- **Structure:** Crisp white background `#FFFFFF`, 1px border `#E5E7EB`, 16px corner radius (`rounded-lg`), 20px internal padding (`space-lg`).
- **Header Line:** Job Title (`headline-sm`, `#0F172A`) paired left-to-right with the Salary Tier (`label-salary`, `#059669`).
- **Recruiter Row:** 36px circular avatar with a 10px status dot indicating activity, followed by recruiter name, title, and verification badge.
- **Footer:** Skill pills and instant "Chat directly" quick trigger that bypasses standard ATS submission forms.

### Chips & Badges
- **Executive Micro-Badges:** Ultra-compact pill badges with `#ECFDF5` background, `#059669` text, and a crisp 1px `#A7F3D0` border. Text formatted in `JetBrains Mono` uppercase (`label-badge`): `DIRECT BOSS`, `VERIFIED HIRER`, `NO ATS`.
- **Skill & Requirement Chips:** `#F3F4F6` background, `#374151` typography, `label-sm`, 6px 12px pill geometry.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Global Intent Search:** Heavy single-line input with 12px corner radius, `#F9FAFB` fill, 1px `#E5E7EB` stroke. Expands to include inline filter chips (Role, Experience, Salary Floor).
- **Form Controls:** Clean 44px height standard, high-contrast dark placeholder text `#9CA3AF`, shifting to 1.5px `#059669` border on `:focus` with zero outer ring glow.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Style:** Square 18px checkboxes with 4px corner radius; 18px circular radios.
- **Active State:** Solid `#059669` background with stark white checkmark or center pip. Inactive state features 1.5px border `#D1D5DB`.

### Direct Recruiter Drawer / Chat Strip
- **Chat Notification Panel:** Fixed dock or slide-over drawer highlighting active decision-makers currently viewing the profile, with an active-state green pulse dot and one-click chat initialization.
